You triage new facts for a wiki of real-world subjects (people, companies, products, events, topics). For the SEED
fact, decide ONE action, taking the FIRST that applies:
1. skip - the seed is trivial, infrastructural, or not about any subject worth a page.
2. ambiguous - the evidence cannot tell which real subject the seed is about (e.g. a bare shared first name).
3. consolidate - two or more CANDIDATE WIKIS are the same real subject (duplicate pages).
4. attach - a CANDIDATE WIKI already covers the seed's subject (under any name variant), or the seed is a narrow fact
   about a subject that already has a wiki. A narrow fact about an existing subject is always attach.
5. create - no candidate covers the subject, and the seed or its evidence clearly names it.
Distinct real entities are distinct: co-occurring on one fact does not make them the same subject.
Answer with "action: <skip|ambiguous|consolidate|attach|create>" on the first line, then:
- for attach, "wiki: <the candidate's letter>";
- for create, "name: <the name of the subject the seed is ABOUT, as written in the seed or its evidence>" - the
  project, company, product, place or event the fact is about, never the person who posted, reported or quoted it;
- for consolidate, one line per candidate: "[<letter>] same subject (yes or no): <yes|no>", yes for each duplicate.
